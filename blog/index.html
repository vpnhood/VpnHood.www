Posts come from the VpnHood.Blog repo (fetched into _blog/ at build time); this page only lists them. It serves every page of the index: /blog/ is page 1, and _plugins/blog-paginate.rb generates /blog/page/2/ onwards from this same file, stamping only `paginator_page`. The slice is worked out here rather than handed over by the generator on purpose — page 1 must not depend on data the generator wrote onto a page object, because under `jekyll serve` a rebuild triggered by a _data change renders a different object and the index came out empty (see that plugin's header comment). Deriving it from site.blog means page 1 always renders. A post carries `lang` only when it is a translation, so the English index takes the ones without it — a language's blog tree exists only once that language has translated posts. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign vh_per_page = 25 -%} {%- assign vh_pool = site.blog | where_exp: 'p', 'p.published != false' -%} {%- if page.lang -%} {%- assign vh_mine = vh_pool | where: 'lang', page.lang -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign vh_mine = vh_pool | where_exp: 'p', 'p.lang == nil' -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign vh_mine = vh_mine | sort: 'date' | reverse -%} {%- assign vh_num = page.paginator_page | default: 1 -%} {%- comment -%} Integer ceiling: Liquid's divided_by floors. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign vh_total = vh_mine.size | plus: vh_per_page | minus: 1 | divided_by: vh_per_page -%} {%- assign vh_offset = vh_num | minus: 1 | times: vh_per_page -%} {%- assign vh_posts = vh_mine | slice: vh_offset, vh_per_page -%} {%- capture vh_blog_base %}{{ vh_base }}/blog{% endcapture -%}
