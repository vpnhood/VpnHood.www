A VPS whose host gives it QEMU's basic kvm64 model hides them all, whatever the hardware. ARM64's minimum did not change. The check greps two of the six flags: kvm64 lacks every one, and a real processor with SSE4.2 and POPCNT has the rest. Its copy is server_cpu.json, shared by the three pages, each key falling back to English: a card with no words in it does not degrade.
